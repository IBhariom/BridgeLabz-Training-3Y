Welcome to Mathura

Mathura is an ancient city in Uttar Pradesh, India. It is famous for its history, temples, culture and beautiful religious destinations.

History of Mathura

Mathura is one of the oldest cities of India. It has played an important role in Indian history and is traditionally associated with Lord Krishna.

The city has been an important centre of art, religion and trade for centuries.

Famous Places

Krishna Janmabhoomi

A famous religious site associated with the birthplace of Lord Krishna.

Dwarkadhish Temple

A beautiful and historic temple located in the heart of Mathura.

Vishram Ghat

A popular ghat situated on the banks of the Yamuna River.

Culture of Mathura

Mathura is known for its colourful festivals, traditional music, dance and local cuisine. Janmashtami and Holi are especially popular celebrations in the region.
