Frontend Development:

The frontend is everything the customer sees and interacts with Homepage with products, Search bar, Product images and descriptions, Shopping cart and Checkout page.

Backend Development:

The backend performs tasks that users cannot see Stores product information in a database, Checks product availability, Processes payments, Manages customer accounts and Sends order confirmation emails.

Full-Stack Development:

A full-stack developer works on both the frontend and backend.

Example: While building the shopping website, a full-stack developer:

->Designs the product page (frontend).
->Creates the API to fetch product details (backend).
->Connects the webpage to the database so products appear correctly.
->Implements user login and order processing.
